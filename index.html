      How to read this

      Four tabs, one decision

      • Where we stand: the plan for a fictional SaaS company. KPI cards, the ARR bridge, 13-week cash against the floor and the covenant, and the three statements.
      • Initiatives: each proposal to spend money, measured alone against the plan: what it costs, when, what it returns, how uncertain that is, and what it does to cash.
      • Strategy: the objective and its guardrails, every initiative ranked, and the best set for a budget, found by running every combination.
      • Briefing: the recommendation in plain words, and saved scenarios side by side.

      Plan and changes

      The plan is the default assumptions with nothing added. "Change vs plan" is your edits and the initiatives you include. A grey dashed line is the plan; blue is the plan with your initiatives.

      Pass, fail and warnings

      Pass keeps a guardrail. Fail breaks it. Fail on a watch line, like the covenant, is a warning that does not decide anything.

      Assumptions and actuals

      Every input is in the Assumptions panel. Assumption marks a guess about the future, Actual a fictional opening fact. When a campaign is marked completed, its actuals can update its channel's rates, and the panel shows which ones moved.

      The integrity badge

      Assumptions

      Every input to the model. Actual marks a fictional opening fact; Assumption marks a guess about the future. Changes recalculate everything at once.
